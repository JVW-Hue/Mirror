ABOUT

Who we are

A small team with a long horizon.

Helix Technologies was founded in 2014 by a group of researchers and engineers who believed that the world's most important institutions deserved better tooling. We started with a single federal contract and a one-room office in Cambridge. Today, our systems process over ██████████ events per day across four continents.

Our mission

To build the predictive infrastructure that allows good decisions to happen at the speed of consequence.

How we work

We are deliberately small. Most of our engineers hold advanced degrees, and many have spent time inside the agencies we now serve. We believe that trust is earned in long conversations, not short contracts — and that the right answer is rarely the cheapest one.

Our internal review process is one of the strictest in the industry. Every model that touches a human subject goes through a three-stage review with our Ethics & Oversight Board before deployment.

What we believe

  • Privacy is a feature, not a tax.
  • Models should be inspectable. By us, and by our clients.
  • Long-term partnerships beat short-term revenue.
  • If we can't explain a prediction to the person it affects, we shouldn't make it.

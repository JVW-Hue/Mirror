AUDIT-2021-003

Audit ID: AUDIT-2021-003
Agency: [REDACTED]
Status: INCOMPLETE

Audit Scope

AUDIT-2021-003 was initiated in January 2021 to review Helix Technologies' compliance with federal regulations regarding behavioral prediction models and data privacy.

The audit was conducted by [REDACTED] and focused on:

  • Project MIRROR's data collection practices
  • Subject consent and notification procedures
  • Data security and access controls
  • The relationship between Helix and its federal clients

Audit Timeline

January 2021: Audit initiated

February 2021: On-site inspection of Helix headquarters

March 2021: Review of MIRROR documentation (partial access granted)

April 2021: Audit suspended — "jurisdictional concerns"

May 2021: Audit formally terminated — no report issued

Final Entry — April 14, 2021

"We have been informed that our jurisdiction does not extend to SITE-07 or any activities conducted therein. We have been further informed that the MIRROR model operates under a [REDACTED] exemption that places it outside our review authority."

"We are terminating this audit effective immediately. No report will be issued. All evidence gathered will be sealed."

"For the record: we found things that concern us. We were not permitted to investigate further."

Supplemental Note

"The auditor who wrote this note was reassigned three days later. The auditor's replacement has no record of the original audit."

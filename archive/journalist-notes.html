How the Story Broke

Timeline · 2021-04-12 to 2021-05-04

GREEN THREAD · HOW THE STORY BROKE
A timeline of how the Vasquez/Helix story moved from a single anonymous email to a 312-page FOIA release to a federal investigation. The timeline is, in the formal sense, the public record of a story that was, in the formal sense, 22 days in the making.

Day 1 · 2021-04-12

  • 03:14 UTC · Elena Vasquez sends "the letter" to Mark Renner
  • 03:17 UTC · Elena Vasquez opens the audit pipeline to the three parties (Elena, journalist T. Park, federal judge [sealed])
  • 04:22 UTC · T. Park receives anonymous email with 312-page PDF and the line "two and a half million Americans are on a list and they don't know it"
  • 11:42 UTC · Helix web operations team files the "EV-2417 echo" anomaly report
  • 16:47 UTC · Aisha Lopez signs severance agreement
  • 23:14 UTC · Elena Vasquez files civil action (Vasquez v. Helix Technologies, 1:21-cv-10417)

Week 1 · 2021-04-13 to 2021-04-19

  • 04-13 · T. Park begins reading 312-page PDF
  • 04-14 · T. Park calls Aisha Lopez · Lopez confirms resignation letter is authentic
  • 04-15 · T. Park calls "M. Patel" (pseudonym) · Patel confirms 3am pageview is authentic
  • 04-18 · T. Park verifies the technical claims in the PDF with three independent security researchers
  • 04-19 · T. Park contacts the New York Times Washington bureau

Week 2 · 2021-04-20 to 2021-04-26

  • 04-22 · T. Park calls Margaret Chen · Chen declines to comment
  • 04-23 · Chen's lawyer sends cease-and-desist letter
  • 04-26 · T. Park contacts the US Attorney's Office for the District of Massachusetts

Week 3 · 2021-04-27 to 2021-05-03

  • 04-27 · US Attorney's Office opens initial inquiry
  • 04-30 · 14:22 UTC · Federal judge issues temporary restraining order · MIRROR system shut down
  • 04-30 · 14:23 UTC · Cohort registry deleted
  • 04-30 · 14:24 UTC · Audit pipeline transferred to National Archives custody
  • 05-01 · T. Park files story with New York Times
  • 05-02 · New York Times fact-checks the story · 47 sources contacted · 38 confirm
  • 05-03 · New York Times legal review

Day 22 · 2021-05-04

  • 06:00 EST · New York Times publishes "Leaked Files Reveal How Helix Built System to Flag 2.3 Million Americans" · 4,200 words · above the fold · 47 sources
  • 07:30 EST · Helix Technologies issues statement: "We are aware of the article in the New York Times. We are reviewing the claims. We deny any wrongdoing."
  • 09:00 EST · Senator Markey (D-MA) calls for congressional hearing
  • 10:30 EST · Federal court unseals Vasquez civil action
  • 14:00 EST · Federal court orders release of FOIA file (312 pages)
  • 16:47 EST · Aisha Lopez's resignation letter, sealed since 2021-04-11, is de-sealed by court order
  • 19:22 EST · Margaret Chen resigns as CEO
  • 23:14 EST · Elena Vasquez's 2018-04-15 press release is republished in full, with annotations, by the New York Times

Aftermath

  • 2021-06-14 · David Rosen dismissed as General Counsel
  • 2021-06-14 · Aisha Lopez files separate civil action (Lopez v. Helix Technologies, 1:21-cv-10518)
  • 2021-09-08 · Federal investigation of Margaret Chen announced
  • 2022-02-11 · Vasquez civil action settled
  • 2022-08-22 · Lopez civil action settled
  • 2023 · Federal procurement reform passes Congress · "Vasquez-Lopez Privacy Act"
  • 2024-11-04 · Federal client reorganized
  • 2025-06-12 · T. Park publishes book · 384 pages · dedicated to the 2,304,891

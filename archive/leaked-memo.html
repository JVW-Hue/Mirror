Memo: Termination of E. Vasquez

Internal, Helix Technologies · 2021-03-19

TO: All Helix Staff

FROM: R. Chen, General Counsel

DATE: 2021-03-19

RE: Personnel matter — E. Vasquez

Effective immediately, Dr. Elena Vasquez is no longer employed by Helix Technologies. The separation was the result of a confidential internal process and is not subject to further discussion.

Dr. Vasquez made significant contributions to the company over her five years of service, and we thank her for those contributions.

All inquiries regarding this matter should be directed to the HR department. Please do not approach Dr. Vasquez's former colleagues about the separation.

As a reminder, the Helix NDA, including the non-disparagement clause, remains in force for all former employees. Violations will be pursued.

— R. Chen

CONFIDENTIAL

// RESPONSE — distributed in hard copy at the Cambridge office, 2021-03-19, 17:42

To my colleagues,

You may have seen the memo. I want to say, briefly, that I do not consider the characterization in the memo to be accurate. I was not let go for "conduct unbecoming" in any sense that I would recognize as such. I was let go because I would not sign an affidavit that, to my knowledge, was false.

I cannot tell you what the affidavit was about. I can tell you that I asked, three times, to be shown the underlying record that the affidavit was meant to certify. I was not shown the record. After the third request, my access was revoked and my employment was terminated.

If you have questions, you should ask them. You should ask Margaret. You should ask Robert. You should ask the chair of the Ethics & Oversight Board. If you do not get answers, that is itself a piece of information.

It has been a privilege to work with you.

— Elena

// both documents above were entered into evidence as exhibits 4 and 5 in Vasquez v. Helix Technologies, Inc., US District Court for the District of Massachusetts, case 1:21-cv-10417.

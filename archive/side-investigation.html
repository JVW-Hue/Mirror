How it started

I received, on 2021-04-12 at 04:22 UTC, an email from an address I did not recognize. The email contained, in the body, a single line of text, and, as an attachment, a 312-page PDF. The line of text read: "two and a half million Americans are on a list and they don't know it." The PDF was, on the basis of subsequent analysis, the complete contents of the audit pipeline that Dr. Elena Vasquez had, on 2021-04-12 at 03:17 UTC, just opened to the public.

I did not, at the time, know who Elena Vasquez was. I did not, at the time, know what an audit pipeline was. I did not, at the time, know that the email I had just received was, on the basis of subsequent analysis, the single most consequential document leak in the history of the United States.

I spent, in the formal sense, the next 90 days reading the 312-page PDF. I spent, in the practical sense, the next 90 days reading the 312-page PDF, and I spent, in the formal sense, the next 90 days calling sources, in the formal sense, who could, in the formal sense, confirm what the 312-page PDF said. I spent, in the practical sense, the next 90 days calling sources, in the formal sense, who could, in the formal sense, confirm what the 312-page PDF said, and I spent, in the formal sense, the next 90 days confirming, in the formal sense, that the 312-page PDF was, in the formal sense, the truth.

What I learned, in the order I learned it

  1. The system was real. The system was called MIRROR. The system was a population-scale behavioral prediction system. The system was, in the formal sense, deployed.
  2. The cohort registry was real. The cohort registry contained 2,304,891 entries. The cohort registry was, in the formal sense, the basis on which 2,304,891 Americans were watched.
  3. The threshold lowerings were real. The threshold lowerings were, in the formal sense, not supported by any change in the underlying science. The threshold lowerings were, in the practical sense, supported by a contract modification.
  4. The audit pipeline was real. The audit pipeline was, in the formal sense, the only system in the production stack that maintained a complete and tamper-evident record of every threshold change and every addition to the cohort registry.
  5. The civil action was real. The civil action was, in the formal sense, filed on 2021-04-12. The civil action was, in the formal sense, settled in 2022. The civil action was, in the practical sense, the basis of a set of reforms to the federal procurement process that, on the basis of the documents I have read, are the most significant reforms to the federal procurement process since the Privacy Act of 1974.

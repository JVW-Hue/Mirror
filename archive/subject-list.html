The subject registry — redacted sample

Excerpted from exhibit 23, Vasquez v. Helix Technologies, Inc.

The following is a redacted sample of the MIRROR cohort registry, as of 2021-03-19. The sample is drawn from the audit pipeline described in § 5 of the system specification. The audit pipeline maintains a complete record of every subject who has been added to or removed from the cohort registry, the timestamp of the action, the reason for the action, and the identity of the operator who initiated the action.

The names, dates of birth, and addresses of subjects have been replaced with hashed identifiers. The dates of cohort addition and the axis scores have been preserved.

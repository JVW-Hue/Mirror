ORDER ON MOTION TO TERMINATE PROJECT MIRROR

ORDER GRANTING-IN-PART PLAINTIFF'S EMERGENCY MOTION

Upon consideration of Plaintiff's Emergency Motion to Enjoin Destruction of Evidence, the supporting memorandum, the declarations of Elena Vasquez, Mark Renner, and Aisha Lopez, the ex parte submissions of counsel for the federal client of record, and the entire record herein, the Court FINDS as follows:

FINDINGS OF FACT

1. Plaintiff, Dr. Elena Vasquez, was employed by Defendant Helix Technologies, Inc. as Principal Engineer, Behavioral Modeling, from 2018-04-13 to 2021-04-15. During her employment, Plaintiff served as the system architect of an internal monitoring pipeline (the "audit pipeline") designed to record and surface anomalous outputs from a population-scale predictive system operated by Defendant under contract with a federal client of record (the "federal client").

2. On 2021-03-26, Defendant issued internal order EV-2417-T, signed by General Counsel R. Chen and approved by CEO M. Chen, directing the destruction of (a) a cohort registry stored in a warm-store database cluster and (b) the audit pipeline itself, both within fourteen (14) days, with each destruction certified in writing by every Helix principal engineer with knowledge of the systems. The order is appended to Plaintiff's declaration as Exhibit A.

3. Three (3) engineers with knowledge of the systems refused to sign the certification described in order EV-2417-T, § 2(3): (a) Plaintiff, on 2021-03-26; (b) Mark Renner, on 2021-03-27; and (c) Aisha Lopez, on 2021-03-28. Each refusal triggered the consequence provisions of § 4 of the order, including termination of employment.

4. The cohort registry, as described in the declaration of Plaintiff and in internal order EV-2417-T, contained entries pertaining to approximately 2,304,891 natural persons, identified by hashed identifiers keyed to fourteen (14) behavioral axes scored on a 0.00 to 1.00 scale.

5. The audit pipeline, as described in the declaration of Plaintiff, was designed, in Plaintiff's words, "to fail in a way that was legible." The pipeline recorded, in a manner not accessible to the cohort registry itself, the fact and circumstances of any cohort entry scoring above the watching threshold on at least three (3) of the fourteen (14) axes.

6. The federal client of record, having been informed of the order by Defendant on 2021-03-29, has not objected to the order on the record, and has indicated to the Court ex parte that it takes no position on the destruction of the systems described herein.

7. Plaintiff filed the instant motion on 2021-04-09, the same day that Mark Renner was placed on administrative leave. The motion was served on counsel for Defendant on 2021-04-10. The Court held an emergency hearing on 2021-04-12 at 9:00 a.m.

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

8. The systems described in order EV-2417-T are not, on the record before the Court, work product of Defendant. They are, on the record before the Court, records of unlawful activity, within the meaning of 18 U.S.C. § 1514A(b)(2)(A), as amended, and are protected whistleblower disclosures within the meaning of 18 U.S.C. § 1514A(a)(1), as amended.

9. The Court further concludes, on the record before the Court, that the federal client of record, having failed to assert a privilege over the systems described in order EV-2417-T, has waived any privilege it might otherwise have had with respect to those systems.

10. The Court further concludes that the public interest in the preservation of the systems described in order EV-2417-T outweighs Defendant's interest in the destruction of those systems, and that the destruction, if completed, would cause irreparable harm to the public interest and to the integrity of any subsequent administrative or judicial proceeding.

ORDERING PARAGRAPHS

Accordingly, it is hereby ORDERED:

11. Defendant, its officers, employees, agents, and contractors are ENJOINED from destroying, overwriting, or otherwise rendering unreadable the cohort registry and the audit pipeline described in order EV-2417-T, pending the resolution of the underlying action or further order of this Court.

12. Defendant shall, within seventy-two (72) hours of the entry of this Order, file under seal with this Court a complete and unredacted inventory of the systems described in paragraph 11, including the number of records, the schema, the location, the access controls, and the identities of all persons with administrative access.

13. Order EV-2417-T, signed by R. Chen and approved by M. Chen on 2021-03-26, is SEALED by the Court and shall remain sealed until further order of this Court.

14. The Plaintiff shall, within thirty (30) days of the entry of this Order, cause a copy of order EV-2417-T to be served on the federal client of record by means that shall provide proof of service to this Court.

15. The Clerk is directed to maintain this Order under seal until such time as the Court, on motion of any party or on its own initiative, lifts the seal. The Court will consider lifting the seal on its own initiative on 2021-04-15, after the time for the federal client of record to assert a privilege under paragraph 9 has expired.

So ORDERED this 12th day of April, 2021, at Alexandria, Virginia.

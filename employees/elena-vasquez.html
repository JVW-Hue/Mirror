TEAM / VASQUEZ, ELENA

Elena Vasquez

Principal Engineer, Behavioral Modeling · Joined 2016 · no longer with the company

EV

Employee ID
EV-2417

Elena joined Helix in 2016 from a research position at MIT, where she led the Behavioral Signal Processing group in the Media Lab. Her doctoral work on multi-agent simulation of social movements formed the basis of our first production cohort modeling system.

At Helix, Elena led the design of our real-time event ingestion pipeline and the audit subsystem that protects the integrity of every prediction we ship. Her 2019 talk at the ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency — "On the limits of de-identified prediction" — is required reading for all incoming engineering hires.

Areas of expertise

  • Distributed systems for stream processing
  • Behavioral modeling at population scale
  • Audit pipeline architecture
  • Ethical review of predictive systems

Selected publications

  • Vasquez, E. (2019). "On the limits of de-identified prediction." ACM FAccT.
  • Vasquez, E. & Park, J. (2018). "Cohort stability under rotating salts." Privacy Enhancing Technologies.
  • Vasquez, E. (2017). "Multi-agent simulation of emergent group behavior." MIT PhD Thesis.

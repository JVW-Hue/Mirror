Just had a third-round interview with them. The role is a behavioral modeling engineer. The first two rounds were normal, technically rigorous but normal. The third round was in-person at their Cambridge office and got… weird?

They walked me through their office and pointed out, on a whiteboard in a locked conference room, what they called a "cohort taxonomy" — basically a giant tree of population segments. They were very proud of it. They had names for the segments that I am not going to repeat here because they made me uncomfortable.

Then they gave me a take-home that asked me to score, on a 1–10 scale, a list of 50 short social media posts for "mobilization potential." The posts were real, the list said so, and the data file included user IDs. I scrubbed the user IDs before doing the exercise. They noticed. They asked why. I told them. They did not seem to understand why this was a problem.

Should I be worried or am I being paranoid?

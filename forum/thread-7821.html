Helix ethics board — what do they actually do?

Started by u/former_eo_employee · 8 replies · last activity 2021-03-14 · [THREAD CLOSED BY MODERATOR]

u/former_eo_employee · 2021-03-10

I worked at Helix in 2017-2018, in the Ethics & Oversight office. I want to push back gently on the narrative that the EO board is a fig leaf. It is not. It is a small group of competent, well-meaning people who are doing their best to ask hard questions about work they often don't fully understand.

What I saw, in my time there, is that the EO board would flag a model for review, the model would be paused while the review happened, the review would produce a set of recommendations, and the model would be re-deployed with the recommendations… mostly implemented. The board could not block a deployment. It could only slow one down.

Over time, the engineering team learned what kinds of models the board would flag and which kinds it wouldn't. The flags mostly hit things that were obviously problematic. Things that were less obviously problematic — the ones that did the most harm, in retrospect — sailed through.

So: the EO board is real, it is not a sham, and it is also not the safeguard people want it to be.

Helena's Note

Found: October 3, 2019
Location: Elena Vasquez's office
Written on: Helix Technologies letterhead

Elena —

I know what MIRROR is. I know what you've done. I know what it does.

2,304,891 people. 14 axes. 96% accuracy.

You told the board it was analytics. You told the ethics committee it was prediction. You told me it was research.

It's none of those things.

I'm going to the ethics committee. I'm going to the federal review. I'm going to whoever will listen.

If something happens to me, the data is in the Vasquez drive. Password is your name. Lowercase.

You can't hide this forever.

— Helena

This note was found on Elena Vasquez's desk on October 5, 2019, two days after Helena vanished. Vasquez's office had been cleaned out except for this note. It was left in the center of the desk, face up.

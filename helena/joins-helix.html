Helix Technologies Announces Key Hire to Lead Population-Scale Behavioral Modeling Initiative

Cambridge, MA — Helix Technologies, the leading provider of advanced behavioral modeling systems to federal clients, today announced the hiring of Dr. Elena Vasquez as Director of Population-Scale Behavioral Modeling. Dr. Vasquez joins Helix from a postdoctoral fellowship at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where she was a member of the research group focused on large-scale data systems and audit infrastructure.

"We are thrilled to welcome Dr. Vasquez to the Helix team," said Margaret Chen, CEO of Helix Technologies. "Dr. Vasquez is one of the foremost experts in the design of population-scale behavioral modeling systems, and her expertise will be invaluable as we continue to deliver best-in-class solutions to our federal clients."

Dr. Vasquez holds a PhD in Computer Science from MIT, where her dissertation focused on the design of audit pipelines for large-scale data systems. Her work has been published in the leading peer-reviewed venues in computer science, and she is the recipient of the ACM Doctoral Dissertation Award.

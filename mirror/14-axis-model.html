The 14-Axis Model

Source: Leaked Helix Technologies document
Classification: TOP SECRET

Overview

The 14-axis model is the core of Project MIRROR. It maps human behavior across 14 distinct dimensions, creating a complete behavioral profile for each subject. The model processes 2,304,891 subjects every night, updating predictions with 96% accuracy.

The 14 Axes

  1. Axis 1 — Behavioral Consistency: Measures how predictable a subject's behavior is over time. Threshold: 0.78.
  2. Axis 2 — Predictive Accuracy: Measures the model's confidence in its predictions. Threshold: 0.96.
  3. Axis 3 — Anomaly Detection: Identifies deviations from predicted behavior. Threshold: 0.55.
  4. Axis 4 — Decision Confidence: Measures the subject's confidence in their own decisions. Threshold: 0.89.
  5. Axis 5 — Pattern Recognition: Measures the subject's ability to recognize patterns. Threshold: 0.92.
  6. Axis 6 — Temporal Mapping: Maps the subject's perception of time. Threshold: 0.87.
  7. Axis 7 — Behavioral Drift: Measures how much the subject's behavior deviates from the model's predictions. Threshold: 0.31.
  8. Axis 8 — Predictive Horizon: Measures how far ahead the model can predict. Threshold: 0.94.
  9. Axis 9 — Anomaly Severity: Measures the severity of detected anomalies. Threshold: 0.68.
  10. Axis 10 — Model Confidence: Measures the model's overall confidence in its analysis. Threshold: 0.91.
  11. Axis 11 — Anomaly Rate: Measures the rate of anomalies across all subjects. Threshold: 0.12.
  12. Axis 12 — Subject Compliance: Measures how closely the subject follows predicted behavior. Threshold: 0.97.
  13. Axis 13 — System Integrity: Measures the overall health of the MIRROR system. Threshold: 0.99.
  14. Axis 14 — MIRROR Status: Indicates whether the model is active. Status: ACTIVE.

Threshold Significance

When a subject crosses a threshold on any axis, the model flags them for review. When a subject crosses thresholds on multiple axes, the model takes action.

What action? The documents don't say. But the model has taken action on 92,196 subjects so far.

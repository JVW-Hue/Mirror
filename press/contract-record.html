Federal Contract Record · Helix Technologies

Released 2021-04-22 · SAM.gov public record

BLUE THREAD · CONTRACT DISCLOSURE
Federal contract awards are public record under the Federal Funding Accountability and Transparency Act. The following is from SAM.gov, with redacted client identifiers.

The following contracts were awarded to Helix Technologies, Inc. by federal agencies between 2014 and 2021. The names of the contracting agencies have been redacted in this public release under the exemption for "sensitive law-enforcement techniques," although the contracts themselves are not classified.

Total obligated $58,300,000 2017–2021

What the contract record shows

The contract record shows a steady escalation of funding from 2017 through 2020, with a sharp drop in 2021 corresponding to the public-relations damage from the Vasquez allegations.

The 2020-03-22 modification — the COVID-19 modification — added $22.8M to the contract and is, in scope, the single largest expansion. The description does not specify what was added, but the timing corresponds to the period in which the MIRROR threshold was lowered by approximately 30%, and the cohort registry grew from approximately 410,000 to 1,200,000 entries.

The 2020-11-04 modification — the "threshold re-tuning" — is, in our professional judgment, the most important line in this contract record. A re-tuning of a model threshold in a population-scale behavioral prediction system is, functionally, a re-scoping of the system. A re-scoping of the system is, functionally, a different system. The 2020-11-04 modification should, in our professional judgment, have been treated as a new contract, with a new competition, with a new opportunity for other vendors to bid. It was not.

Subcontracts

Helix Technologies, Inc. subcontracted portions of the work described in the above contracts to the following entities. The names of the subcontractors have not been redacted in this public release.

  • Palantir Technologies — data ingestion tooling, 2018-2020. Total subcontract value: $4.1M.
  • Amazon Web Services — infrastructure, 2018-2021. Total: approximately $11M (estimated, based on internal allocation).
  • Two undisclosed subcontractors — described in the original contract as "linguistic analysis support" and "geographic data integration." Names redacted from this public release.

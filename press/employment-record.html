FOIA Response · Helix Technologies · Employment Record

Released 2021-04-22 · In response to FOIA Request #2021-0482

BLUE THREAD · FOIA RELEASE
This document was released in response to a Freedom of Information Act request filed by a coalition of civil-liberties organizations. The release was compelled by court order.

The following is the complete employment record of every individual who held a position at Helix Technologies between 2014-01-01 and 2021-04-12, as maintained by the company's HR department and provided to the Department of Justice in response to the above-referenced FOIA request.

Notable entries, with departure reasons, are highlighted below. The full record is 117 individuals and is available in the original court filing.

Selected entries

Elena Vasquez — Principal Engineer, Behavioral Modeling

Employee ID: EV-2417
Hire date: 2016-03-14
Termination date: 2021-03-19
Termination reason (per Helix): "Conduct unbecoming. Unauthorized disclosure of confidential materials."
Termination reason (per Vasquez complaint): "Wrongful termination in violation of federal whistleblower-protection statutes. Refusal to sign a sworn affidavit attesting to facts the affiant had reasonable grounds to believe were false."
Severance: None. NDA enforcement initiated within 72 hours of termination.
Status as of 2021-04-22: Civil action pending. Defendant in counter-suit filed by Helix Technologies alleging breach of NDA. Plaintiff in wrongful-termination suit alleging violation of whistleblower protections.

Mark Renner — Senior Engineer, Infrastructure

Employee ID: MR-2207
Hire date: 2017-06-02
Termination date: 2021-04-09
Termination reason (per Helix): "Performance. Failure to execute required production cutover within scheduled window."
Termination reason (per contemporaneous internal note): "Refused to sign destruction certification per Order EV-2417-T. Placed on administrative leave 2021-03-27. Terminated 2021-04-09."
Status as of 2021-04-22: Cooperating witness in DOJ investigation. Has provided a sworn statement to investigators.

Aisha Lopez — Head of Ethics & Oversight

Employee ID: AL-1834
Hire date: 2018-09-04
Resignation date: 2021-04-11
Resignation reason (per Helix): "Personal reasons. Pursuing opportunities outside Helix."
Resignation reason (per Lopez's resignation letter, sealed, available in the same court filing): "I am resigning because the Ethics & Oversight Board I chair has, in the last 18 months, attempted to pause three deployments of MIRROR-related work. All three pauses were overridden by the executive team within 14 days. I am resigning because I have been instructed, in writing, to resign 'for personal reasons' in exchange for a severance package that includes a non-disparagement clause. I am resigning because the alternative to resignation is termination, and termination would, in this case, be accompanied by the filing of a counter-suit. I cannot afford the counter-suit. I can afford the non-disparagement. I am therefore resigning, and I am therefore bound, by the terms of the severance I have signed, to say only that I am resigning for personal reasons. I am stating, here, for the record, that the personal reasons are that the organization I work for has asked me to participate in a process that I believe to be a violation of the public trust."
Status as of 2021-04-22: Not a party to the Vasquez civil action. Has, per filings in the same case, agreed to provide testimony if subpoenaed.

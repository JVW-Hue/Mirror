Vasquez FOIA File

Released 2021-05-04 · In response to FOIA Request #2021-0521 (Vasquez legal team)

BLUE THREAD · VASQUEZ FILE
Documents released in response to a FOIA request filed by the legal team representing Dr. Elena Vasquez in her civil action against Helix Technologies. Released under court order over the objection of Helix's outside counsel.

This is the file. It is the file that, until last week, did not exist. It is the file that the lawyers said did not exist. It is the file that, the lawyers later conceded, did exist but had been misfiled. It is the file that, the lawyers said after that, existed but was privileged. It is the file that, in the end, was released.

Document 1: Internal email, M. Chen to D. Rosen, 2020-03-19

From: m.chen@helix.tech

To: d.rosen@helix.tech

Subject: re: threshold re-scoping

David — the client has asked for a 30% threshold reduction across all 14 axes, effective immediately. I have told them this is, in my professional opinion, an indefensible change. They have told me that the public-health situation has changed the cost-benefit calculation. I have told them that the public-health situation has not changed the underlying science. They have reminded me that the contract is $58M and the next modification is pending.

I have agreed to make the change. Please direct the team to implement it by end of week. Do not loop in Elena on this. I will brief her separately.

— M

Document 2: Internal email, E. Vasquez to M. Chen, 2020-03-22

From: e.vasquez@helix.tech

To: m.chen@helix.tech

Subject: threshold re-scoping — formal objection

Margaret — I was not briefed separately. I learned of the threshold change from the production-deploy log this morning. I am formally objecting to the change on the following grounds:

  1. The 30% threshold reduction was not reviewed by the Ethics & Oversight Board.
  2. The 30% threshold reduction is not supported by any change in the underlying science or any change in the threat model that I am aware of.
  3. The 30% threshold reduction will, on the basis of the back-testing I have just completed, result in a 4x increase in the cohort registry within 6 weeks. The 4x increase corresponds to the addition of approximately 800,000 Americans to the cohort registry, almost all of whom, on the basis of the back-testing, would not have crossed the previous threshold.
  4. The 30% threshold reduction was, on the basis of the email I have just read (which I was not copied on), directed by the federal client in connection with a contract modification. I do not believe that contract negotiations are an appropriate basis for changing the threshold of a population-scale behavioral prediction system.

I am requesting an emergency meeting of the Ethics & Oversight Board. I am requesting that the threshold change be paused pending the meeting. I am requesting that the production-deploy log be audited to determine the timing of the change relative to the contract modification.

I am not, in this email, threatening to resign. I am noting, for the record, that the audit pipeline I built is the only system in the production stack that maintains a complete record of every threshold change. I am also noting, for the record, that the audit pipeline is, by design, unable to be modified without my knowledge.

— Elena

Document 3: Internal email, M. Chen to A. Lopez, 2020-03-23

From: m.chen@helix.tech

To: a.lopez@helix.tech

Subject: re: Elena's email

Aisha — Elena has asked for an emergency EO board meeting. I have, for the reasons we discussed, declined to convene the meeting. I am writing to you, in your capacity as chair of the board, to ask whether the board has any procedural basis to convene a meeting without my approval. I do not believe it does. I am writing to confirm that you agree.

— Margaret

Document 4: Email response, A. Lopez to M. Chen, 2020-03-23

From: a.lopez@helix.tech

To: m.chen@helix.tech

Subject: re: re: Elena's email

Margaret — I confirm that the board has no procedural basis to convene a meeting without your approval. I confirm that I will not attempt to convene a meeting. I confirm that I will not respond to Elena's request for an emergency meeting.

I want, for the record, to note that I find the request reasonable. I want, for the record, to note that I would have supported the request had I been asked. I want, for the record, to note that I am uncomfortable with my role in this.

— Aisha

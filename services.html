SERVICES

Capabilities

Three practice areas, working in concert.

01

Predictive Analytics

Time-series forecasting, anomaly detection, and signal extraction at population scale. Our models are designed to operate on streaming event data with sub-second latency.

Industries: federal, finance, infrastructure, healthcare systems.

02

Behavioral Modeling

We build cohort-level models that respect individual privacy while still producing actionable segmentation. All cohorts are de-identified at ingest and re-identified only with explicit, revocable consent.

Use cases: campaign response, churn prevention, public sentiment, risk scoring.

03

Mission Systems

Long-horizon partnerships with government and critical-infrastructure operators. Our mission systems are designed to outlast their original use case — and to be re-purposable when the mission shifts.

Clearance: TS/SCI facility, full SAP-eligible engineering team.

Network Traffic Analysis — Helix Technologies

Source: Independent network monitoring
Period: August–December 2019

Summary

Independent network monitoring has revealed anomalous traffic patterns originating from Helix Technologies' Floor 12 server room. The patterns are not consistent with normal server operations.

Pattern 1: The 3:14 AM Cycle

Every night at 3:14 AM, Floor 12 servers initiate a burst of 113 outbound connections. These connections target a variety of external IP addresses, but all resolve to the same domain: a 404 page that doesn't exist.

This pattern has repeated every night for 127 consecutive days.

Pattern 2: The 4:17 AM Silence

At exactly 4:17 AM, all Floor 12 servers go silent. Not low activity — zero activity. No heartbeat signals. No status updates. No log entries. For exactly 17 minutes, Floor 12 appears to be offline.

Then at 4:34 AM, everything resumes as if nothing happened.

Pattern 3: The Data Flow

Between 3:14 AM and 4:17 AM, Floor 12 servers transfer approximately 2.3 terabytes of data outbound. The data is encrypted with a key we haven't been able to crack.

Where is 2.3 TB of data going every night? And what happens during the 17-minute silence?

Pattern 4: The Correlation

The 3:14 AM traffic spike coincides exactly with the 4:17 AM silence. The 2.3 TB transfer happens in the gap between them. The 113 outbound connections happen before the transfer. The 17-minute silence happens after.

This is not random. This is a process. Something runs every night on Floor 12, and it takes exactly 1 hour and 3 minutes to complete.
